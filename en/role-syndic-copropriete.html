What does a co-ownership syndic do?

The syndic runs the building on behalf of all the co-owners. It carries out the decisions of the general meeting, keeps the accounts and makes sure the common parts are maintained.

Keeping the co-ownership's accounts

The syndic prepares the budget, sends calls for funds, pays suppliers, chases late payments and presents the accounts to the general meeting each year. The co-ownership's money must be tracked separately from the syndic's own. How charges are calculated.

Organising the general meeting

The syndic calls the meeting, prepares the agenda and the documents, writes the minutes, then carries out what was voted: contracts, works, budget, works fund. Our guide to the general meeting.

Maintaining the building and supervising works

Cleaning, lift, heating, insurance, minor repairs: the syndic manages the contracts and steps in when there is a problem. For larger works, the syndic gathers quotes, puts them to the vote and oversees the work on site.

Managing the works fund

Since 1 August 2023, co-ownerships in Luxembourg have paid into a works fund every year. The syndic calculates the contribution, calls for it and tracks the amount held for each unit. Our guide to the works fund.

Keeping records and informing owners

Co-ownership rules, minutes, contracts, invoices, plans: the syndic keeps the building's records and must be able to show them to the co-owners. At mäisyndic, everything can be viewed online, in each person's language. What the co-ownership rules are for.

Volunteer or professional?

In some small buildings, a co-owner acts as volunteer syndic. It is possible, but it is a lot of work: bookkeeping, meeting notices, works fund. Our Volunteer+ plan leaves them in charge and takes care of the paperwork.
