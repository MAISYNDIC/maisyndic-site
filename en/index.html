The situation

Small buildings, big obligations.

Many co-ownerships in Luxembourg have only around ten flats. They have the same obligations as large residences, without the same means.

The works fund is mandatory

Since 1 August 2023, every co-ownership must pay into it each year. Calculation, calls for funds, tracking per unit: we handle it.

Small buildings are often neglected

High minimum fees, slow replies, extra charges. At mäisyndic, a 6-unit building gets the same care as a 60-unit one.

Being a volunteer syndic is a second job

Bookkeeping, notices, reminders, insurance… We take it all over, or support you if you want to stay syndic.

Your questions

Are you a fully professional syndic?

Yes. BUSINESS EVOLUTION SARL holds business permits as an estate agent and as a property manager – syndic de copropriété (nos. 10124351/2 and 10124351/3), and the guarantees required by Luxembourg law.

How does switching syndic work?

The switch is voted at a general meeting. We prepare the resolution, then collect the records, accounts and contracts from your previous syndic. See the steps.

Our building has fewer than 10 units. Are you interested?

That's our core business. Working online lets us manage small co-ownerships well, at a fair price.

Is the building's money kept separate from yours?

Yes. Each co-ownership has its own bank account, and its transactions can be viewed in the online portal.
