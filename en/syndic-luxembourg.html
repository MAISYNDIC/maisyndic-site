Co-ownership syndic in Luxembourg City and across the country

Our office is at 51 rue de Strasbourg, in Luxembourg-Gare. Because we work online, we manage co-ownerships in every municipality of the Grand Duchy, with the same prices and the same response time.

Luxembourg City

Gare, Bonnevoie, Hollerich, Belair, Limpertsberg, Kirchberg, Merl, Cessange, Gasperich, Cents, Hamm, Beggen, Eich, Dommeldange, Rollingergrund, Mühlenbach, Pfaffenthal, Clausen, Grund, Weimerskirch: we are a few minutes from every district of the capital.

The South

Esch-sur-Alzette, Differdange, Dudelange, Pétange, Sanem, Schifflange, Bettembourg, Kayl, Rumelange, Mondercange, Käerjeng, Mondorf-les-Bains and neighbouring municipalities.

The Centre and the West

Strassen, Bertrange, Mamer, Hesperange, Walferdange, Steinsel, Sandweiler, Niederanven, Leudelange, Kopstal, Kehlen, Steinfort, Mersch, Lintgen, Lorentzweiler.

The North and the East

Ettelbruck, Diekirch, Wiltz, Clervaux, Vianden, Redange, Echternach, Grevenmacher, Remich, Junglinster, Mertert, Wormeldange.

How we work remotely… and on site

The admin is done online: calls for funds, statements, documents and the tracking of requests are available to every co-owner in their portal. But a syndic must also come on site: we hold the annual general meeting, visit every building at least once a year and come out whenever needed.

A licensed syndic, one price list

BUSINESS EVOLUTION SARL holds business permits as an estate agent and as a property manager – syndic de copropriété (nos. 10124351/2 and 10124351/3). Whether your building is in Kirchberg or in Wiltz, the price per home and the extras are the same, and they are written into the contract. Get a quote.
