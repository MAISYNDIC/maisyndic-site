What does a co-ownership syndic cost in Luxembourg?

A syndic's price is made up of an annual flat fee and services billed separately. Here is how to read a quote, what makes it vary and the questions to ask before the vote.

An annual flat fee, usually calculated per unit

Most syndics in Luxembourg charge for day-to-day management as an annual flat fee. It is calculated per unit (each flat, cellar or parking space counts, depending on the co-ownership rules) or set for the whole building, often with a minimum per building.

In principle, this flat fee covers the bookkeeping, calls for funds, follow-up of suppliers and contracts, the annual general meeting and management of the works fund. Check the exact list: two flat fees at the same price can cover very different things.

What is often charged as an extra

Some services don't apply to every co-ownership, so they are billed separately. The most common are:

  • taking over the file when you change syndic, especially if the accounts are behind;
  • supervising major works voted at the meeting, often as a percentage of the cost;
  • an extraordinary general meeting in addition to the annual meeting;
  • handling a claim with the building's insurance;
  • the file given to the notary when a unit is sold;
  • recovering unpaid charges through the courts.

These lines sometimes cost more than the flat fee. Ask for them to be priced in the contract, before the vote.

What makes the quote vary

The number of units is the first criterion, but not the only one. A lift, communal heating, a water meter whose costs must be shared out, many maintenance contracts or long-standing arrears all need more follow-up. The state of the accounts left by the previous syndic also matters: accounts that have to be rebuilt take time.

How to compare two quotes

  • Is the price annual or monthly, excluding or including VAT?
  • What exactly does the flat fee include? How many general meetings? How many building visits?
  • Does each extra service have a written price, or is it ‘price on request’?
  • What response time does the syndic commit to?
  • Can co-owners view the accounts and documents online?
  • Does the syndic hold the business permit as a property manager – syndic de copropriété?
