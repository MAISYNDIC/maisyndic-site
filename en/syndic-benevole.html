Volunteer syndic: you stay in charge, we do the paperwork

Are you an owner and the syndic of your building? With Volunteer+, you remain syndic and we handle the admin.

Who is it for?

In Luxembourg, an owner may act as syndic without a business permit if the building has no more than 9 residential units and they own one of them. Volunteer+ is made for these buildings.

What we do

  • Bookkeeping and annual statement
  • Budget and calls for funds
  • Works fund calculation and tracking
  • Notices and minutes prepared, ready to sign
  • Deadline reminders: meeting, insurance, contracts
  • Online portal for all owners

What stays with you

  • The syndic mandate and the decisions
  • Contact with neighbours and tradespeople
  • Signing the documents

And when you want to step down?

We take over with the Essential plan, with no change of tools or contact person.
