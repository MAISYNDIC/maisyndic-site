The works fund, explained simply

Since 1 August 2023, every co-ownership in Luxembourg must build up a works fund (fonds de travaux). Here are the essentials.

What is it?

A cash reserve the co-ownership builds up over time to pay later for the upkeep, repair and improvement of the building, including energy renovation.

Who is concerned?

Every co-ownership, whatever its size. The obligation was introduced by the law of 30 June 2022 amending the law of 16 May 1975 on co-ownership, and has applied since 1 August 2023.

How much must be paid?

The law sets a minimum annual contribution per square metre of energy reference area, depending on the thermal insulation class of the energy performance certificate (EPC): €3 for classes A+ to C, €5 for D and E, €6 for F to I or without a valid EPC. The general meeting may decide to pay more.

What happens when you sell?

The amounts paid stay with the unit: they are not refunded to the seller. When a unit is sold, we tell the notary its situation, including the amount held for it in the fund.

What mäisyndic does

We calculate your building's minimum contribution, include it in the calls for funds, keep the money in a separate account and show the amount held for each unit in the online portal.
