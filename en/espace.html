What you will find in your portal

Your account at a glance

What you owe, your calls for funds with their PDF notice and payment QR code, your payments. Owners' council members can also see the building's invoices.

Notifications on your phone

Call for funds, reply to your report, incident in the building, general meeting: you are notified, with no amount or name on the lock screen.

A question for the meeting

Ask online, within the legal deadline, for a question to be added to the agenda: all co-owners are informed.

Organised documents

Minutes, statements, contracts and rules, always at hand.

Report with a photo

A leak, a broken light? Report it in 30 seconds and follow the repair.

Direct debit

Your SEPA mandate is ready in your portal: fill it in, sign it and send it back to your managing agent, and your calls for funds are then collected automatically.

Meetings prepared online

Notice, appendices and proxies available in your portal.

In your language

Français, Deutsch, English, português: everyone gets documents and replies in their own language.

Selling or letting your flat?

mäisyndic is also a licensed estate agent. We already know your building, its accounts and its works fund: your sale file is ready within 72 hours, at a preferential rate if you sell with us.
