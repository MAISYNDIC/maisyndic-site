Privacy

The sections "Which data?" to "Who receives it?" concern this website's quote form. For the co-owners of the buildings we manage, see also "Assistant, sorting of requests and takeover of buildings by artificial intelligence". "Who is responsible?" and "Your rights" apply to everyone.

Who is responsible?

BUSINESS EVOLUTION SARL, 51 rue de Strasbourg, L-2561 Luxembourg. Contact: contact@maisyndic.lu.

Which data?

Only what you enter in the quote form: name, email, phone, details about your building and your message.

Why?

To answer your request and prepare a quote. We don't use it for anything else and we don't sell it.

How long?

Three years after our last exchange if you don't become a client. It is then deleted.

Who receives it?

Only our team. The form is processed directly by our website, hosted by OVHcloud in the European Union, with no third-party service.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu. You can also contact the National Commission for Data Protection (CNPD).

Assistant, sorting of requests and takeover of buildings by artificial intelligence

For the co-owners of the buildings we manage: to suggest a first sorting of reports, to answer questions asked in the online space and, when a building is entrusted to us, to prepare its takeover, mäisyndic uses an artificial intelligence service (Claude) provided by Anthropic (Anthropic Ireland, Limited), acting as a processor within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR.

  • Reports: the title, location, description and photos of the report (without their metadata), the "common areas" and "urgent" boxes, your language, the name and town of the co-ownership, and the list of our agency's tradespeople (name and trade) are sent. We do not add your name, e-mail address or telephone number: please avoid writing them in the report. A member of our team reviews every suggestion: nothing is decided or sent automatically.
  • Assistant (optional): your question, your latest exchanges with the assistant and the information about your account already visible in your space are sent: account holder, name and address of the co-ownership, units, balance, calls for funds, direct debit, general meetings, documents, service charge statements, owners' council membership, your reports and those you marked "I'm affected too", your property manager's contact details.
  • Takeover of a co-ownership: when a building is entrusted to us, the documents handed over by the previous property manager and needed for the takeover (list of co-owners, ownership shares, statement of accounts, bank statements, minutes, contracts…) are sent in full, to prepare the co-ownership's file. They notably contain the co-owners' names, addresses, e-mail addresses and telephone numbers, their units, shares and balances. A member of our team checks and corrects everything before the co-ownership is created: nothing is created automatically.

Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. This data is stored and processed in the United States. This transfer outside the European Union is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, included in Anthropic's data processing agreement (DPA); you can obtain a copy by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu. We keep the log of requests (questions, answers and suggestions) for 12 months at most in the application, then for at most 12 months in our encrypted backups. For a takeover, this log only keeps the number of documents read and their cost: what the artificial intelligence read is deleted as soon as the co-ownership is created (the documents then join the co-ownership's documents, visible to our team only), or at the latest 12 months after they were uploaded if the takeover does not go ahead. Anthropic deletes this data from its servers within 30 days, except where required by law or where needed to combat breaches of its usage rules: for example, an exchange flagged by its automated checks is kept for up to 2 years, and the score from that check for up to 7 years.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in handling requests about the management of your co-ownership quickly and in taking over the management of a building entrusted to us without data-entry errors. You can object to this processing by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu: your requests are then handled without artificial intelligence, for all the accounts we know to be yours (tell us about them if you have several). The association of co-owners can also ask for the takeover of its building to be carried out without artificial intelligence.

Cookies and third parties

This site uses no advertising cookies and no analytics. Fonts are hosted on our own server: no data is sent to third parties when you browse the site.
