Privacy

The sections "Which data?" to "Who receives it?" concern this website's quote form. For the co-owners of the buildings we manage, see also "Management of your co-ownership", "Assistants, sorting of requests, e-mails, invoices, quotes, contracts, reports, agendas, mail, notary questionnaires, transfers, accounts and statements, meter readings, translations and takeover of buildings by artificial intelligence" and "Online space: sign-in and notifications". "Who is responsible?" and "Your rights" apply to everyone.

Who is responsible?

BUSINESS EVOLUTION SARL, 51 rue de Strasbourg, L-2561 Luxembourg. Contact: contact@maisyndic.lu.

Which data?

Only what you enter in the quote form: name, email, phone, details about your building and your message. To limit abuse (5 requests per hour), the server also keeps a hash of your IP address, with the time of your requests, for one day at most; this file never contains the address itself. As with any website, our hosting provider also records visits (IP address, page requested) in its technical logs. Anonymous visitor statistics are described below (“Cookies, audience measurement and third parties”).

Why?

To answer your request and prepare a quote. We don't use it for anything else and we don't sell it. If you ask for a quote for at least two units, you immediately receive by email the quote and the draft contract as PDFs, pre-filled with your details; we receive a copy. If you ask for a quote, we may write to you at most twice to ask whether you have any questions (about a week, then a month after your request), as long as you have not told us your decision; each email contains a link to stop receiving them. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request and our legitimate interest in following up a quote request; you can refuse these reminders at any time.

How long?

Three years after our last exchange if you don't become a client. It is then deleted.

Who receives it?

Only our team. The form is processed directly by our website, hosted by OVHcloud in the European Union, with no third-party service. To follow up quote requests and send these two reminders, they are also passed to our management application, also hosted by OVHcloud in the European Union; its encrypted backups pass through GitHub (United States, certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu. You can also contact the National Commission for Data Protection (CNPD).

Management of your co-ownership

For the co-owners of the buildings we manage, mäisyndic is the controller of the data it processes to carry out the tasks entrusted to it by the law of 16 May 1975 on co-ownership and its implementing regulation of 13 June 1975: list of co-owners, accounting, calls for funds, general meetings, archives, as well as for the tools it provides (online space, artificial intelligence). For processing decided by the general meeting (for example video surveillance), the association of co-owners is the controller and mäisyndic acts on its behalf.

  • Data: your name, contact details and language, your units and ownership shares, the status of your account (calls for funds, payments, and your IBAN if you have signed a direct debit mandate), your participation in general meetings, your reports and the e-mails you send to our address for co-owners (kept for 12 months after receipt, then deleted).
  • Legal basis: the property manager's legal obligations and, for the rest, our legitimate interest in performing the mandate given to us by the association of co-owners; for a direct debit, the performance of the direct debit mandate you signed.
  • Who receives it: our team; our hosting provider OVHcloud (European Union); for any work, the company in charge of it, for the part that concerns it; for a direct debit, the banks involved; the other co-owners, as provided for by law and the life of the building (minutes of general meetings, which show how each person voted; name of the person who had an item added to the agenda; shared incident, without the name of the person who reported it); members of the owners' council (the co-ownership's invoices); for the sale of a unit, the notary; in case of legal action, the bailiff or the lawyer; the postal service for registered letters; GitHub (United States, registered under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), through which our encrypted backups and technical logs pass.
  • How long: during our mandate, for as long as the management of the co-ownership and our legal obligations require: the co-ownership's accounts and supporting documents for 5 years after the end of the financial year, after which they are destroyed only with the association's consent; the minutes of general meetings for as long as the co-ownership exists. At the end of our mandate, the co-ownership's archives are handed over to the next property manager.

Assistants, sorting of requests, e-mails, invoices, quotes, contracts, reports, agendas, mail, notary questionnaires, transfers, accounts and statements, meter readings, translations and takeover of buildings by artificial intelligence

For the co-owners of the buildings we manage: to suggest a first sorting of reports, to answer questions asked in the online space, to sort the e-mails we receive and suggest a reply, to prepare the entry of suppliers' invoices, to translate the agenda of general meetings into your language, to prepare its items from the quotes received, to suggest who a transfer received without a reference belongs to and, when a building is entrusted to us, to prepare its takeover, mäisyndic uses an artificial intelligence service (Claude) provided by Anthropic (Anthropic Ireland, Limited), acting as a processor within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR.

  • Reports: the title, location, description and photos of the report (without their metadata), the "common areas" and "urgent" boxes, your language, the name and town of the co-ownership, and the list of our agency's tradespeople (name and trade) are sent. We do not add your name, e-mail address or telephone number: please avoid writing them in the report. A member of our team reviews every suggestion: nothing is decided or sent automatically. Do not write any information about your health in your reports or questions.
  • Assistant (optional): your question, your latest exchanges with the assistant and the information about your account already visible in your space are sent: account holder, name and address of the co-ownership, units, balance, calls for funds, direct debit, general meetings, documents, service charge statements, owners' council membership, your reports and those you marked "I'm affected too", your property manager's contact details.
  • Takeover of a co-ownership: when a building is entrusted to us, the documents handed over by the previous property manager and needed for the takeover (list of co-owners, ownership shares, statement of accounts, bank statements, minutes, contracts…) are sent in full, to prepare the co-ownership's file. They notably contain the co-owners' names, addresses, e-mail addresses and telephone numbers, their units, shares and balances. A member of our team checks and corrects everything before the co-ownership is created: nothing is created automatically.
  • Suppliers' invoices: to prepare the entry of an invoice in the co-ownership's accounts, we send the invoice, the name and address of the co-ownership, and its expense accounts and allocation keys (never our list of suppliers, nor any co-owner data). An invoice may mention a person, for example the occupant at whose home work was carried out. A member of our team checks everything and enters the invoice themselves: nothing is recorded automatically. If you are a tradesperson or supplier working under your own name, you can object to this reading by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu: your invoices are then entered by hand.
  • Agenda of general meetings: so that you receive, under the French text of your notice of meeting, a translation into your language (German, English or Portuguese), the title and draft resolution of each item are sent, and nothing else (neither the name of the co-ownership nor the list of co-owners). An item may mention a person, for example a co-owner asking for permission to carry out works; if you have objected to the use of artificial intelligence, an item that names you is translated by hand. A member of our team reviews, corrects and approves each translation: a translation that has not been approved does not appear in any document. The translation is for information only: the French version alone is authoritative. After the meeting, your online space also offers a summary of the decisions in your language, based on these translations; only the minutes in French are authoritative.
  • Quotes for general meetings: to prepare an agenda item from the quotes received (suggested title, draft resolution, majority and allocation key), we send these quotes, the name and address of the co-ownership and its allocation keys, never any co-owner data. A quote may mention a person, for example the occupant at whose home work is planned. A member of our team checks everything, chooses the majority and adds the item themselves: nothing is added automatically.
  • Transfers received without a reference: when the application cannot work out by itself who a transfer received belongs to (reference, known account, name or amount), our team may ask the artificial intelligence for a suggestion. We then send the date, amount, payer's name, description and reference of the transfer (never the account number), and the list of the building's co-owners with what they owe. If you have objected to the use of artificial intelligence, you are not on this list, and a transfer that appears to come from you is not sent. A member of our team checks and allocates the transfer themselves.
  • E-mails sent to our address for co-owners: to reply faster and in your language, our team may ask the artificial intelligence to sort an e-mail, flag an emergency and suggest a reply. We then send the subject and text of the e-mail (we do not add your e-mail address or your attachments). A member of our team reviews, completes and sends the reply themselves. If you have objected to the use of artificial intelligence, your e-mails are handled without it, and any suggestion already made is deleted.
  • Year-end accounts and the letter accompanying your service charge statement: before closing the accounts, our team may ask the artificial intelligence for a commentary, and have it draft and translate a letter explaining the financial year. Only totals per expense item (amount, budget, previous year), the points to check found by our software (amounts, items) and, for the letter, the name of the building and of our agency and our instructions are sent: no data about you. A member of our team reviews and approves the letter, which is enclosed with your statement in your language; your share of the charges is still calculated by our software.
  • Heating and hot water meter readings: when the metering company sends us its readings as a PDF or photo, our team may have them read by the artificial intelligence, which only takes the unit numbers and consumption figures (the document may mention the occupants' names). A member of our team checks every line before it is saved; the document is not kept. If an owner concerned has objected to the use of artificial intelligence, the readings are entered without it.
  • Questions from our team about your building: to find information quickly, a member of our team may ask the artificial intelligence a question about a building. We then send the question and a summary of the building prepared by our software, which may include your name, your balance and any late payments. Nothing is changed, and neither the question nor the answer is kept. If you have objected to the use of artificial intelligence, you do not appear in this summary.
  • Building contracts: so as not to miss a termination date, our team may have a contract of the building (maintenance, insurance, inspection) read by artificial intelligence. The contract and the name and address of the building are then transmitted, with no other data about the owners. A contract may however name a person, for example the chair of the owners' council who signed it. A member of our team checks everything and records the contract themselves; reminders and the comparison of invoices with the contract price are done by our software, without artificial intelligence. If you are a contractor working in your own name, you can object to this reading by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu.
  • Recurring reports: to spot a fault that keeps coming back (the same garage door several times a year) and suggest a lasting repair, our team may ask artificial intelligence to review the building's reports from the last two years. For each report, the month, title and location are then transmitted, without your name, the description or the photos. If you have objected to the use of artificial intelligence, your reports are not transmitted.
  • Agenda and management report: to prepare the general meeting, our team may ask artificial intelligence to suggest agenda items and to draft the property manager's management report. A summary of the year prepared by our software is then transmitted, without any owner's name (account totals, contracts, recurring problems, arrears as a total only, decisions of the last meeting). A member of our team chooses the items, reviews and approves the report before it is attached to the notice of meeting.
  • Incoming mail: to file paper mail received for your building (bailiff, authorities, insurer, notary) and not miss a deadline, our team may have a scanned letter read by artificial intelligence. The letter and the list of the buildings concerned (name, address) are then transmitted. A letter may name you, for example a bailiff's letter. A member of our team checks and files the letter themselves; it remains visible to our team only. If you have objected to the use of artificial intelligence, a letter naming you is handled without it.
  • Notary's questionnaire when selling: if you sell your unit, our team may have the notary's questionnaire read by artificial intelligence. This questionnaire usually names you and the buyer. It comes with a summary prepared by our software, without your name (amounts due and to come for the units sold, works fund, any recovery proceedings, meeting decisions, insurance). The questionnaire is not kept; a member of our team checks, completes and approves each answer. If you have objected to the use of artificial intelligence, as the seller or as a buyer who already owns a unit in a building we manage, the questionnaire is completed without it.

Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. This data is stored and processed in the United States by Anthropic's parent company, Anthropic, PBC. Anthropic may also process it in other countries for safety reviews, support or incident response. For the United States, the European Commission's adequacy decision only applies to companies registered under the "EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework", which is not the case for Anthropic, PBC. This transfer is therefore covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914), supplemented by an assessment of the risks arising from US law; you can obtain a copy by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu. We keep the text of requests (your questions, the assistant's answers and the sorting suggestions) for 6 months at most in the application, so that we can respond to a complaint, detect abusive use and answer your access requests, and their technical data (date, cost, model used) for 12 months at most. Our encrypted backups, which are used only to restore the application after an incident, may still contain them for 12 months at most; if a backup ever had to be used, your objections and erasure requests would be applied again before the application is put back into service. For a takeover, we only keep the number of documents read and their cost: what the artificial intelligence read is deleted as soon as the co-ownership is created (the documents then join the co-ownership's documents, visible to our team only), or 12 months after the file was last changed if the takeover does not go ahead. For an invoice, we likewise only keep the number of invoices read and their cost: what the artificial intelligence read stays only, encrypted, in the session of the person entering the invoice, for as long as the entry takes; an invoice that is entered joins the co-ownership's accounting records, and an invoice that is not entered is deleted after 24 hours. For a translation, we likewise keep only the number of requests and their cost; the translations stay with the meeting's agenda, in the co-ownership's records. For a quote, we likewise keep only the number of readings and their cost: what the artificial intelligence read stays only, encrypted, in the session of the person preparing the agenda; a quote attached to the notice of meeting joins the co-ownership's documents, and a quote that is not attached is deleted after 24 hours. For a transfer, we likewise keep only the number of requests and their cost: the suggestion stays only, encrypted, in the session of the person handling the bank statements. For an e-mail, the assistant's suggestion is kept, encrypted, with the e-mail and deleted with it after 12 months. Anthropic deletes this data from its servers within 30 days, except where required by law or where needed to combat breaches of its usage rules: for example, an exchange flagged by its automated checks is kept for up to 2 years, and the score from that check for up to 7 years.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in handling requests about the management of your co-ownership quickly, in keeping your co-ownership's accounts without data-entry errors, in taking over the management of a building entrusted to us without data-entry errors, in preparing the agenda of general meetings without errors, in following the building's contracts without missing a deadline, in maintaining the building efficiently, in preparing complete general meetings, in handling the building's mail on time, in answering the notary without errors when a unit is sold, in allocating payments received without errors, in replying quickly to your e-mails and in informing you, in a language you understand, of the decisions put to the general meeting. We have weighed these interests against your rights; the document setting this out can be sent to you on request at contact@maisyndic.lu.

You can object to this processing by writing to contact@maisyndic.lu: your requests are then handled without artificial intelligence, for all the accounts we know to be yours (tell us about them if you have several). The association of co-owners can also ask for the takeover of its building to be carried out without artificial intelligence.

Online space: sign-in and notifications

For the co-owners of the buildings we manage:

  • Sign-in: without a password, through a link sent to your e-mail address. This e-mail also contains a 6-digit code, to sign in from the app installed on your phone or from another device. The link and the code work only once (the code for 15 minutes at most); we keep only a one-way hash of them, and they are useless once used or expired. The records of these requests (dates, hashes) are deleted after 30 days; wrong codes are counted for 24 hours per e-mail address and per IP address, so that nobody can guess them. During your visit, your session keeps the IP address and the type of browser used; so that you stay signed in on that device, a sign-in cookie is kept for at most 400 days and deleted when you sign out.
  • Notifications (optional), on your phone or computer: they are offered to you in your space; if you accept them (your device asks for your consent once), your browser gives us a technical address specific to that device, with two encryption keys. We use it only to let you know about a reply or completed work on your reports and those you said also concern you, an incident in the building, a new call for funds or a general meeting (notice, minutes), and, at your request, for a test notification. The short message contains no amount and no name. It is encrypted, then delivered by your device's notification service, chosen by your browser or your phone (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, which may be located outside the European Union): that service receives the device's technical address, the time of sending and the fact that the message comes from mäisyndic, along with the encrypted message, which it cannot read; we ask it not to keep the message for more than 24 hours if the device is switched off. Google and Microsoft are registered under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework; for Apple, the company responsible in Europe is Irish; Mozilla (Firefox) processes this data in the United States without an adequacy decision or safeguards: the US authorities could ask Mozilla when your device receives our notifications (never their content). That is why turning them on asks for your explicit consent ("I agree, turn them on" button). This address is deleted when you turn notifications off or sign out on that device (if you had accepted them, they are turned back on the next time you sign in on that device, without asking again); otherwise as soon as the service reports that the device no longer receives them or its keys turn out to be unusable, and at the latest 13 months after your last visit to your space on that device. Your browser also keeps, on that device only, your choice (notifications accepted or turned off) and the date on which you chose "Later" (the offer then comes back after 14 days), linked to an encoded identifier (neither your name nor your e-mail address); this information is not sent to us and stays until you clear the site's data in your browser.

These data may remain for up to 12 more months in our encrypted backups. Legal basis: for sign-in, our legitimate interest in giving you secure access to your space; for notifications, your consent (and, for Firefox's service located in the United States, your explicit consent to this transfer), which you can withdraw at any time from your space or your device's settings. The usual e-mails and letters continue in all cases.

Cookies, audience measurement and third parties

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. To know which pages are useful, our site records each page view anonymously: the time, the page, the site you came from (its domain name only, for example google.com), the page language, whether you use a phone or a computer, and certain actions (using the price calculator, clicking our phone number, e-mail address, Google profile or Facebook page, sending a quote request, without its content). We record neither your IP address nor any identifier: there is no way to recognise you from one visit to the next. These statistics stay on our OVHcloud server in the European Union and are deleted after 25 months. If your browser asks not to be tracked (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), nothing is recorded.

Fonts are hosted on our own server: no data is sent to third parties when you browse the site.
