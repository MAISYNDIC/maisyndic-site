Switching syndic

It's simpler than people think. Here is how it works with mäisyndic, step by step.

  1. 1

    You ask for a quote

    Tell us the number of units, the municipality and what isn't working today. You get a firm quote within 48 hours.

  2. 2

    We meet your owners' council

    By phone, video call or on site. We compare our contract with your current syndic's line by line, so everyone sees the difference.

  3. 3

    You vote at the general meeting

    We prepare the appointment resolution and an explanatory note for the meeting notice, in your owners' languages.

  4. 4

    We take over the file

    As soon as we are appointed, we request the records, accounts, funds and current contracts from the previous syndic. You have nothing to do.

  5. 5

    Your portal opens

    Every owner receives their login in their own language. The first call for funds and the year's calendar follow.

What helps us prepare your quote

  • The number of units and the building's address
  • The latest general meeting minutes
  • The latest service charge statement
  • Your current syndic's contract, if there is one
  • The list of current maintenance contracts

None of this is required for a first price: the number of units and the municipality are enough.

Your building no longer has a syndic?

It happens, especially in small buildings. We help you appoint a syndic and put the co-ownership back in order: accounts, works fund, insurance and contracts.
